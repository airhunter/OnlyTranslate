CHAPTER ONE

Attention and Memory

Attention is not a spotlight that simply reveals what is already there. It changes the shape of what we remember.

注意力并非只是在照亮已经存在的事物。它会改变我们记忆的形状。

When we read slowly, ideas have time to gather around one another. A sentence becomes more than information; it becomes part of a larger pattern.

当我们放慢阅读速度，想法便有时间彼此连接。一句话不再只是信息，而会成为更大脉络的一部分。

“Understanding grows through connection, not accumulation.”

理解来自连接，而不是简单的堆积。

That is why returning to the exact place matters. Reading is a conversation continued over time.

因此，准确回到上次的位置很重要。阅读是一场跨越时间、不断继续的对话。
